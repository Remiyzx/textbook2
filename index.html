Grammar Focus

1. Sentence with Adjectival Predicate

Subject + adv (hěn / yě / dōu / bù) + Adjective

Positive Form

Tā hěn máng.

He is very busy.

Negative Form

Wǒ bù máng.

I am not busy.

With "dōu" (both/all)

Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo.

They are both/all fine.

📌 Enumeration Comma

Used strictly to separate items in a list (similar to list commas in English).

Example: Nǐ bàba、māma hǎo ma?

👥 Plural Marker "men"

ONLY used for human pronouns or person-related nouns (wǒmen, nǐmen, tāmen). Never used for objects or animals!

Grammar Focus

2. Expressing Want & Answering "yào...ma?" Questions

Question Pattern

Nǐ yào kāfēi ma?

Do you want coffee?

Positive Answer

Yào.
Wǒ yào kāfēi.

Yes, I want coffee.

Negative Answer (Tone Change!)

Bú yào.
Wǒ bú yào kāfēi.

No, I don't want coffee. (bù → bú)

📊 Chinese Word Order Structure Chart

Subject (siapa) Time Word (bila) Adverb (adv) Verb (buat apa) Object / Predicate
wǒ / nǐ / tā jīntiān / míngtiān yě / dōu / bù qù / chī / hē / yào kāfēi / kělè
wǒmen / tāmen zuótiān dōu hěn -- (Adjective Predicate) -- hǎo / máng

Wrap-up

🧱 Key Sentence Structures

Subject + adv + Adjective

Wǒ bù máng. / Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo.

Subject + yào + Object

Wǒ yào kāfēi. / Wǒ bú yào kělè.

🎵 Essential Tone Change Rules

Tone of "bù"

bù máng (4th tone before 2nd tone)

bú yào (changes to 2nd tone before 4th tone!)
