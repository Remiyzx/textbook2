💡 Extra Vocabulary Note: Romantic Partner vs. Male/Female Friend

Male friend / Female friend (Just a friend / Not dating)

Sentence with Adjectival Predicate

Subject + adv (yě / dōu / bù / hěn) + Adjective

Positive Form

Tā hěn máng.

He is very busy.

Negative Form

Wǒ bù máng.

I am not busy.

With "dōu" (both/all)

Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo.

They are both/all fine.

📌 Enumeration Comma

Used strictly to separate items in a list (similar to commas in English, but only connects related words/names).

Example: Nǐ bàba、māma hǎo ma?

👥 Plural Marker "men"

ONLY used for human pronouns or person-related nouns (wǒmen, nǐmen, tāmen). Never used for objects or animals!

Expressing Want & Answering "yào...ma?"

Question Pattern

Nǐ yào kāfēi ma?

Do you want coffee?

Positive Answer

Yào.
Wǒ yào kāfēi.

Yes, I want coffee.

Negative Answer (Tone Change!)

Bú yào.
Wǒ bú yào kāfēi.

No, I don't want coffee. (bù → bú)

Core Grammar Summary

🧱 Adjectival Predicate (S + adv + A)

Wǒ bù máng. / Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo.

Adjectives act directly as predicates! Adverbs (yě, dōu, bù, hěn) must go directly before the adjective.

👥 The Adverb "dōu" (both / all)

Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo. / Wǒmen dōu hē kāfēi.

  • • Refers back to the plural subject(s) preceding it (wǒmen, tāmen, bàba、māma).
  • • Always placed immediately before the verb or adjective predicate (S [plural] + dōu + V/A).
  • • When combined with other adverbs: yě dōu (also both/all) or dōu hěn (all very).

🥤 Asking & Expressing Wants (yào)

Q: Nǐ yào kāfēi ma?

A: Yào. / Wǒ yào kāfēi. ➔ Positive

A: Bú yào. / Wǒ bú yào kāfēi. ➔ Negative

⚠️ Tone Sandhi of "bù"

bù máng vs bú yào

"bù" changes from 4th tone to 2nd tone (bú) before another 4th tone syllable.
